Perimeter

The total length around a shape's border
Watch the insect walk the perimeter
Perimeter Formulas
Square
P = 4a
All four sides are equal, each of length a.
Equilateral Triangle
P = 3a
All three sides are equal, each of length a.
Rectangle
P = 2(a + b)
Length a and width b — two of each.
Special Case
The square formula P = 4a is a special case of the rectangle formula P = 2(a + b).

When a = b:  2(a + b) = 2(a + a) = 2 × 2a = 4a

The Staircase Insight

Why the step count never changes the perimeter
Press Animate to see the proof
⟶ Horizontal treads (blue)
All horizontal step-edges together span exactly the total width: W = 9 cm. They add up to W no matter how many steps there are.
↕ Vertical risers (orange)
All vertical step-edges together span exactly the total height: H = 6 cm. They add up to H regardless of step count.
So the perimeter is always…
P = W + H + W + H
  = 2(W + H)
  = 2(9 + 6) = 30 cm
General rule
This works for any number of steps. As long as the total width and height stay the same, the perimeter stays the same: P = 2(W + H).
SECTION 6 / 10

Perimeter of Circles

Circle, semi-circle and sector — when the boundary curves
Circle with radius r
Perimeter Formulas
Circle
P = 2πr
Also called the circumference. r is the radius; π ≈ 3.14159.
Semi-circle
P = πr + 2r
Half the circumference (πr) plus the straight diameter (2r).
Sector (angle θ°)
P = (θ/360)×2πr + 2r
Arc = (θ/360)×2πr, plus two radii r each. In radians: P = θr + 2r.
Special Case
A semi-circle is a sector with θ = 180°:

arc = (180/360)×2πr = πr
P = πr + 2r  ✓
